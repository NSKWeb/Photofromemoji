Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority

🔒 100% Client-Side Processing

The Ultra HD Photo to Emoji Converter processes all images entirely in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, stored in any database, or transmitted over the internet in any way. All image processing happens locally on your device.

🗑️ No Data Storage

We do not store your images, processing results, or any personal data. When you refresh the page or close your browser, all data is immediately and completely cleared from memory. Nothing is saved to localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or any other persistent storage mechanism.

📋 No Data Collection

We do not collect, track, or store any personally identifiable information. We do not require account registration, email addresses, or any personal details to use our service. You can use the tool anonymously without providing any information.

🍪 Minimal Cookies

We use only essential cookies necessary for basic website functionality (such as maintaining your session preferences). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that track your behavior across websites.

🌐 No Third-Party Sharing

Since we don't collect or store any data, we have nothing to share with third parties. Your images and personal information are never sold, rented, or shared with any third-party services, advertisers, or data brokers.

🔐 Encryption & Security

Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all connections. However, since your images never leave your device, there's no risk of interception during transmission. Your data stays on your device at all times.

📱 Mobile Privacy

The same privacy protections apply on mobile devices. Your photos are processed locally on your phone or tablet and are never transmitted to any server.

🔄 Temporary Processing

During conversion, your image data exists only in your browser's temporary memory (RAM). Once the conversion is complete or you navigate away, this memory is freed. No permanent files are created on your device.

👁️ No User Monitoring

We do not monitor, record, or analyze how you use the tool. We don't track which images you convert, what settings you choose, or how you use the exported results.

✅ Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data:
• You have no account to delete or manage
• No data is retained that you need to request removal of
• No tracking to opt out of
• Your privacy is protected by design

🔗 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

👶 Children's Privacy

Our service is suitable for users of all ages. Since we don't collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

📜 Changes to This Policy

Our commitment to privacy is fundamental to our service. We will not change this policy to compromise your privacy. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revision date.

📞 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through our contact page.
